# Data pulls

A log of every data pull: re-checking in-flight missions against JPL
Horizons and agency news, and scanning for new launches. Newest first.

Pulls are done by hand, not on a schedule. `docs/TODO.md` shows when the
last one was; once it's more than 10 days old, a "data pull due" item
appears there (regenerated by `tools/refresh_check.py --todo`).

**How to do a pull:** follow `docs/project-transfer.md` §3.4. In short:

1. `tools/.venv/bin/python tools/refresh_check.py --out report.md` and
   work through the report.
2. Scan for new launches (Wikipedia's `List of spaceflight launches in
   <months> <year>` page, then agency pages).
3. Re-fit or extend anything that changed; verify with
   `check_horizons.py` / `check_orbit_phases.py`.
   Low priority, a quick look each pull: has a freely licensed image
   appeared for any link-only gallery tile (assets with `imagePage`, no
   `localImage`; currently Kamoʻoalewa from Tianwen-2)? Usually it comes
   through an open-access (CC BY) paper by the mission team. If so, add
   it with `tools/audit_images.py`'s `MANUAL`.
4. Record it:
   `tools/.venv/bin/python tools/refresh_check.py --update-baseline --log "what changed" --pr "#N"`.
   This adds a row below, resets the Horizons baseline and refreshes
   the to-do block.
