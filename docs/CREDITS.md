the credit and licence each source asks for. Cite sources here whenever
something new is added: a map, an image, a dataset, a constant taken from

BY or BY-SA), the attribution must also be visible in the app, next to
the item, not only in this file.

NASA asks that it be credited as the source and that its images not be
used to imply endorsement ([NASA media usage guidelines](https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media)).

ASU, USGS) are credited as the original sources ask.

## Body and mission images (info panel galleries) ✅

Photos of bodies, spacecraft and rockets in `data/images/bodies/`,
`data/images/missions/` and `data/images/rockets/`, referenced from the
`assets` blocks in `data/bodies/info.json` and `data/flights/*.json`.

**Audit status (2026-11-25, `tools/audit_images.py`):** all 130
gallery images verified: each has its original file (a NASA Image and
Video Library record or a Commons file page), creator and licence in
`data/images/credits.json`. The app shows the creator and licence under
each gallery tile, linked to that page, which also has the
full-resolution original.

- **Sources:** NASA material comes from the NASA Image and Video Library
  (images.nasa.gov) where it's there. Wikimedia Commons is used for
  ESA, ISRO, CNSA, UAE and SpaceX images, and for recent NASA images not
  yet in the library (Lucy, DART, SDO); those are NASA's own files
  mirrored there.
- **NASA library rule:** an item credited to someone other than NASA (a
  contractor, a university) isn't treated as public domain; the tool
  marks it unverified so its terms get checked.
- **Licences in use:** public domain (NASA and US government), CC0,
  CC BY, CC BY-SA and GODL-India. The in-app credit line is what
  satisfies BY and BY-SA.
- **Replaced 2026-11-25:** 27 images whose source couldn't be confirmed,
  or whose source was a fair-use (non-free) Wikipedia file (ExoMars TGO,
  Hope, Tianwen-1). Each local copy is a rendition of at most 800 px of
  the named original.
- **Link-only tiles:** when the only good image of something isn't
  freely licensed, the app doesn't carry a copy. Its gallery tile shows
  no picture and links straight to where the image is published,
  credited, with the asset fields `imagePage` and `imageCredit` instead
  of `localImage`. In use for 469219 Kamoʻoalewa: the only close-up is
  CNSA/Xinhua's Tianwen-2 image (2 July 2026), which has no open licence.
  Open-access papers from the mission team may later publish one under
  CC BY, the way the Tianwen-1 image here was; that's checked during
  each data pull (`docs/data-pulls.md`).

Corrections the visual matcher can't make go in `MANUAL` at the top of
`tools/audit_images.py`.

## Mission facts 📝

Launch dates, arrivals, payloads, status notes and "why it matters" text
come from agency mission pages (NASA, ESA, JAXA, ISRO, CNSA, UAE Space
Agency, Roscosmos), NASA's Space Science Data Coordinated Archive (NSSDCA,
https://nssdc.gsfc.nasa.gov/nmc/), and Horizons for dates. The per-flight
`infoUrl` links point at the mission's page. Per-fact citations are not
yet recorded in the flight files; new flights should note their main
sources in the flight's `statusNote` or a `_sources` field.

## Software

The app itself has no third-party runtime libraries. The developer tools
use numpy, Pillow, matplotlib, Playwright and hapsira (see
`tools/requirements.txt`).
