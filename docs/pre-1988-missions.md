# Interplanetary missions before 1988 (not yet in the catalog)

The catalog starts with Phobos 1 and 2 (July 1988). This is the backlog
of earlier missions that left Earth orbit for somewhere other than the
Moon. Earth–Moon-only missions are excluded. Compiled 2026-11-23 from
memory of the standard record (NASA NSSDCA, mission histories).
**Check each date against NSSDCA when adding the flight**, and record the
source in the flight's `statusNote`, the way the current catalog does.

## Firsts

- **First spacecraft to escape Earth:** Luna 1 (2 Jan 1959). It missed the
  Moon and went into solar orbit, but it was a lunar mission, so it's
  excluded here.
- **First dedicated interplanetary probe:** Pioneer 5 (11 Mar 1960), a
  solar-orbit space-environment probe. It returned data until 26 Jun 1960.
- **First attempt at another planet:** Mars 1M No. 1 (10 Oct 1960), lost
  to a launch failure.
- **First spacecraft to pass another planet:** Venera 1 (launched 12 Feb
  1961). Contact was lost in cruise; it drifted past Venus at about
  100,000 km in May 1961.
- **First successful planetary mission:** Mariner 2, which flew past
  Venus on 14 Dec 1962.

## Notes for adding them

- **Venus landers are the short-ground-mission case:** Venera 7–14 and
  Vega 1–2 lasted 23–127 minutes on the surface. The timeline already
  draws a sub-pixel `ground` bar at a 5 px minimum, with a landing marker
  on top. Pioneer Venus 2's Day probe (67 min) is the same case.
- **Several missions have no JPL Horizons ephemeris.** Use Lambert legs
  between the launch date and the encounter date, as the early catalog
  entries do. Check which of these Horizons does carry (Pioneer 10/11,
  Voyager 1/2, Giotto, ICE, Helios probably do).
- **Voyager 1/2 and Pioneer 10/11 need system tours** for their Jupiter
  and Saturn flybys (and Voyager 2's Uranus and Neptune flybys) to show
  in those system views. Uranus and Neptune have no system view yet.
- **Halley's Comet** isn't a catalog body yet. Vega 1/2, Giotto, Suisei,
  Sakigake and ICE all need it, and ICE needs Giacobini–Zinner.
